Mission, topic boundary, target user, and IA follow this site's Notion Site Control Page.

## Foundation scope

EMFLS Moving helps people organize general moving preparations around the move date, from roughly D-30 through the first week. Its minimum public experience is a six-phase timeline with practical, adaptable prompts and links to clear trust pages.

The site does not interpret leases or law, resolve disputes, recommend moving vendors, claim unverified prices or deadlines, or collect a user's address, contract, or moving schedule. Changes to this scope require a new owner directive and Site Control Page update.
